ROCKET LEAGUE COMMENTARY SCRIPT BANK — BROADCAST REWRITE
400 original spoken lines: 40 situations, 10 variations each.

Copy only the text after the ID into your speech generator.
Keep the ID as the audio filename, for example save_01.wav.
Delivery notes and usage notes are not spoken dialogue.
Team and player names are deliberately omitted. Existing IDs are preserved.
Use each category's ten variations before repeating one within a game.

PERFORMANCE GUIDE
Speak as though watching the play, not reading an announcement. Use contractions,
uneven sentence lengths and natural breaths. Routine touches need interest, not a shout.
Let anticipation rise before a dangerous play; let the result release that tension.
An exclamation mark marks emphasis, not a requirement to scream every word.
For big goals and multiple saves, project with clear consonants rather than distortion.
Analysis and banter should sound like a thought offered to a co-caster, not a tutorial.
These lines are original, inspired by the supplied broadcast's pacing and tactical focus.
Do not carry over its sponsors, event history, named players or transcription errors.

CONTEXT GUIDE
Select a line only when its category is confirmed. A shot is not a goal; a touch in
the air is not automatically a reset. A bump or demo does not prove the net is open.
Double/triple-save calls describe the defensive sequence, not necessarily one player.
Late-goal calls are for meaningful late scores, not an inconsequential consolation.
Overtime winners settle a game; they do not automatically settle a series.
Match-win calls below celebrate a completed game, without inventing a tournament title.
Tactical and banter lines make general observations, not claims about unseen player stats.
