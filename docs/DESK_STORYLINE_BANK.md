# Additional analyst storyline recordings

Three original variants for each of the 100 supplied situations, using the approved ElevenLabs voice. These augment the previous 194 recordings.

Runtime playback is offline. The explicit bake script is the only part that calls ElevenLabs; it reads a key from the process environment. No key is embedded in scripts, manifests, receipts or audio.

Situations that require untracked evidence are recorded but do not enter automatic selection. A numeric OVR alone does not establish a dynasty, a veteran retirement, fan opinion, arena support, or a roster dispute. Between-game stories (77–90) are kept in the series slot and cannot appear in the pre-match selector.
