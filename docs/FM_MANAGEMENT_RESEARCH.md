# Football Manager research and RLM adaptation

Research date: 28 October 2026. This is an original esports adaptation, not a reproduction of Sports Interactive's proprietary formulas. Numerical tuning below is RLM-specific, not an asserted FM spawn rate or algorithm. Official manuals and developer articles are the evidence base; forum anecdotes are not treated as verified mechanics.

## Evidence and design implications

| Area | What the primary source establishes | RLM adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Youth scouting | [Official FM26 youth recruitment guide](https://www.footballmanager.com/the-dugout/top-tips-youth-recruitment-fm26): staff judging ability/potential, adaptability and regional knowledge matter. Ongoing youth focuses, ages 15–21, are one suggested method; manual searches in youth competitions can complement them. | Age and skill briefs, persistent regional coverage and paid recruitment staff. Rare unsigned 15–16-year-olds are a specific RLM design choice, not a universal FM rule. |
| Scouting knowledge | [FM24 recruitment manual](https://community.sports-interactive.com/sigames-manual/football-manager-2024/transfers-recruitment-and-scouting-r4962/): focus criteria, coverage, budget and staff determine access; repeated observation improves reports. A typical comprehensive report needs several observed matches. | Separate discovery from assessment; retain dated reports and deepen them. Calendar observation in RLM is an abstraction, not a claim that simulated amateur matches have been played. Attribute briefs narrow the pool and can yield no results. |
| Potential and development | [Official youth-intake explanation](https://www.footballmanager.com/the-dugout/developing-and-maximising-your-youth-intakes-fm26): facilities/coaching influence intakes; personality affects subsequent development. [Player manual](https://community.sports-interactive.com/sigames-manual/football-manager-2024/players-r4958/) describes training, coaching, facilities and meaningful playing time. | Store a persistent ceiling per generated player; scouting estimates may over/underestimate it and remain uncertain even at full knowledge. Do not automatically give high-potential prospects excellent personality or a fast growth rate. No guaranteed 99-rated outcome. |
| Youth identity and rarity | Developer material discusses both real players and newgens, with variation and random elements in intakes. It does not publish an exact universal wonderkid generation probability. | Clearly fictional grassroots identities, saved once per career/year. Five ordinary candidates per region, 2.5% of candidates with an exceptional ceiling is initial RLM tuning only and requires balance review. No guaranteed elite player per region/year. No special wonderkid badge or discovery email. |
| Squad dynamics | [FM24 dynamics manual](https://community.sports-interactive.com/sigames-manual/football-manager-2024/your-squad-team-report-and-dynamics-r4957/) separates cohesion, atmosphere and managerial support; hierarchy, social groups, happiness and promises matter. | Display cohesion, morale and trust separately. Identify influential voices from tenure/reputation/professionalism, not OVR. Team Play training and retained lineups slowly build cohesion; arrivals require settling. Full social-group simulation is a separate future layer, not implied by this summary. |
| Sporting project and agents | [Official agent feature](https://www.footballmanager.com/features/introducing-intermediaries-and-offloading-players) describes preliminary availability discussions, release clauses, contract expectations and agents' market feedback. The player manual recommends opening discussions to reduce breakdowns and build relationships. | Retain existing project/role/reputation refusals and enforce saved backend negotiating patience with a cooldown. An acceptable wage does not override sporting refusal, duration, role or financial checks. Fictional agencies have persistent per-save relationships shared across clients and basic negotiation styles. They are not claims about real-world representation. |
| Finances | [FM24 finance manual](https://community.sports-interactive.com/sigames-manual/football-manager-2024/finances-r4964/) distinguishes cash, budgets, wages, liabilities, projections and instalment commitments. Deferred cash payment is not free spending capacity. | Existing debt/interest, wage limits, transfer fees, bonuses, contributions and sell-on obligations remain. Add paid staff, reserve guidance and recurring-cash forecasts excluding uncertain prizes. Instalment offers and separate spending allocations require a dedicated settlement engine; never present them as implemented without it. |
| Club structure | [FM24 staff manual](https://community.sports-interactive.com/sigames-manual/football-manager-2024/staff-r4959/) explains staff roles, suitability and delegated responsibilities. | Three functional backroom departments: Recruitment, Coaching and Player care. Approved searches take seven days and incur monthly wages; benefits feed real scouting/training/recovery. Named staff contracts and delegated autonomous transfers are not represented by department levels. |
| Board expectations | [FM24 managerial manual](https://community.sports-interactive.com/sigames-manual/football-manager-2024/football-manager%E2%84%A2-2024-managerial-home-and-profile-r4955/) describes long-term club visions supported by seasonal objectives and multi-area appraisal. | Preserve existing financial recovery safeguards. Staff expansion is constrained by stature and cash. Negotiated annual sporting, youth-selection and wage objectives now sit under a persistent three-year reputation direction. Saved proposals, board counters and bounded negotiations lead to binding wage ceilings; annual measured reviews adjust confidence and wage budgets, with no arbitrary dismissal. Board personalities and takeovers remain separate work. |
| Tactics and familiarity | [Player manual](https://community.sports-interactive.com/sigames-manual/football-manager-2024/players-r4958/) ties familiarity to repeated exposure and warns against constant drastic changes. | Easy/Medium local ground wrappers have explicit support depth, outlet width, demo target and boost reserve. They preserve native aerials, kickoffs and emergencies. Hard/Rocket Host policies cannot be rewritten through these controls. Do not claim a neural bot has learned a new skill or tactical system. |

## Guardrails and balance

- Prospect identities, attributes and ceilings are saved in the individual career database. Reloads must not regenerate them. Existing real identities and already differentiated attributes are preserved.
- Youth uncertainty remains larger than adult uncertainty. Better recruitment staff and repeated regional coverage improve assessments; they never reveal a true hidden ceiling or guarantee success.
- Anonymous prospects must not leak through global player/free-agent searches, exact-name scouting, direct profile links or direct negotiations before discovery. Discovery grants access, not full knowledge.
- No separate paid wonderkid search, guaranteed elite reward or unearned stat growth. Recruitment returns ordinary players too; a failed search is a valid outcome.
- All fees and financial commitments are surfaced before a decision. Successful transactions commit together; failed transactions cannot partially move a player or debit cash.
- New gameplay must be tested for save isolation, repeat calls, reload exploits, stale reports, transfer-window enforcement, staff billing and tactical output changes.

## Remaining larger systems

The work here is an integrated foundation, not "all of Football Manager." More detailed agent motives, training mentorship, staffing delegation, youth competition fixtures and supporter politics still need their own rules and tests. They must not be advertised as complete because a menu or narrative mentions them.


## Board and transfer agreements (2026-10-28)

- Finances now offers a saved board negotiation. Sporting series-win targets, actual registered U22 selections, and wage ceilings trade off against one another. Three low-value proposals exhaust countering; the board proposal can still be accepted. Signed objectives cannot be changed until the 365-day review. A three-year reputation goal persists between annual reviews. Ten completed career series are required before sporting/youth judgements. Reviews adjust confidence and the wage limit by at most 5%; the existing financial-dismissal safeguards remain.
- Incoming transfers have a separate selling-club negotiation before agent discussions. Key-player status, listing, contract expiry and cash distress influence the seller's stance. Counters and withdrawals are saved, ownership-checked and expire after seven days; reopening the page does not reroll them. Existing outgoing sale negotiations remain in use.
- Fixed-price negotiated purchases support full cash or 3/6/12 monthly instalments, with 30% minimum upfront and stricter seller-specific cash requirements. A delayed package can demand a premium. These negotiated packages exclude additional sell-on/buyback/wage-contribution changes during personal terms; the older full-cash clause API remains compatible.
- Every future dollar is recorded by career and deal. Calendar-month dates clamp to month end; rounding conserves the full fee. Only upfront cash moves at signing, while transfer valuation records the whole guaranteed fee. Obligations survive resale/expiry, appear in forecasts and the finance ledger, and settle transactionally once, including missed dates.
- The board checks each future payment date against current recurring income, wages, existing instalments and proposed wages before registration. Forecast income is an estimate, not a guarantee. Later financial deterioration does not forgive debts: seller payment reduces buyer cash and feeds the existing recovery process.
- Existing sell-on rights are settled at resale; deferred deals require enough seller cash plus upfront proceeds to fund those rights. No fee, contract, roster or schedule change survives a failed registration transaction.

These rules are original, explicit RLM adaptations of the official FM finance and club-vision concepts cited above. They are not Sports Interactive's private valuation or board formulas.
