# National management

Open **National team** in Manager Career to accept a national job alongside your club role. Choose three eligible players and save the squad before FIFAe selections lock. National call-ups, tactics and results are separate from club contracts, budgets, squads and RLCS Worlds records.

Eligibility uses the player's recorded nationality. A French player at a foreign club can represent France; a foreign player at a French club cannot. Missing or ambiguous nationality is excluded. GB/UK alone cannot establish England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland eligibility; explicit association codes are accepted. This models the database's single recorded nationality, not undocumented dual citizenship or nationality changes.

Countries need three recorded eligible players to appear in the job selector. Acceptance of a job does not grant a FIFAe invitation. The existing career selection system invites host Saudi Arabia and the strongest fifteen other national squads. It is labelled a career edition of the published 2025 finals format, rather than an exact recreation of current qualifying. Selections and player availability come from this save's player database.

At tournament selection, the manager's chosen three players replace that country's provisional lineup. The country must independently earn its invitation. Squads stay locked for the tournament; national jobs cannot be changed during an active FIFAe event. Tactical changes are available between matchups.

The calendar stops for outstanding national matches. Open the national match screen, select the player to control and play in Rocket League using the shared match bridge. Native introductions, portraits, scoreboard, loading cover and commentary use the national team names and selected players. Both group games must be completed, even after a first-game win. A 2–0 win earns three points; a 1–1 draw earns each country one point. Knockout matchups are best of seven. Results are saved from the bridge and can be recovered through the match screen.

Other countries' matchups are simulated as the calendar advances. A managed country's games are never automatically simulated. National match statistics are stored with the FIFAe tournament; they do not create club appearances, club income or club development rewards. FIFAe winners receive national honours and the existing trophy ceremony flow.

This first implementation covers appointment, resignation, national selection, separate tactics and playable FIFAe finals. Association interviews, national scouting assignments, dual citizenship adjudication and international friendlies are not modelled.
