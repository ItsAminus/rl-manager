# Player introduction scripts

All 30 clips use My Esports Commentator. Names and sourced facts are separate recordings.

## Openings

1. Before we get underway, let's meet the players. First into the spotlight, a player looking to put their stamp on this series.
2. Next in the starting lineup, another player ready to make their presence felt. Here's who to keep an eye on.
3. And completing the first team's lineup, our third player takes the spotlight. Let's take a look at their story.
4. Now, across the field, it's time to meet the opposition. Their first player steps into the spotlight.
5. Next up, another name in this starting three. A fresh series, and another opportunity to make an impact.
6. And finally, completing our introductions, the sixth player in today's matchup. Let's meet them before we head to kickoff.
7. The camera turns to another player in today's lineup. Let's put a name to the car before the action begins.
8. Every series brings a new challenge. Here is another player preparing to take it on.
9. The opening kickoff is getting closer. For now, the spotlight belongs to this member of the starting lineup.
10. Before the cars start moving, let's take a moment to introduce another player in this matchup.
11. Another car, another name to follow. Here is a closer look at the player behind it.
12. Our introductions continue with another member of the starting six. Let's bring them into focus.
13. The stage is set for a fresh series. Here is one of the players who will help decide how it unfolds.
14. There is plenty of Rocket League ahead. First, let's get to know another player taking the field.
15. As both teams prepare for the opening game, we turn our attention to another name in the lineup.

## Closings

1. Now comes another opportunity to add to that story.
2. We'll see what they bring when this series gets underway.
3. That's our first starting three. Now, let's meet the players across the field.
4. A fresh challenge awaits, and it starts with this opening game.
5. Another player to watch as these two teams prepare to face off.
6. All six players introduced. Two teams, one series. Let's get this underway!
7. The introduction is complete. Their next chapter begins when the ball is in play.
8. Now we wait to see how they approach this opening game.
9. That is the player behind the car. Soon, the Rocket League will do the talking.
10. The numbers tell part of the story. This series will give us the next part.
11. Their place in the lineup is set. Now comes the challenge on the field.
12. A new matchup means new decisions to make. We will see how they handle them.
13. There will be opportunities to make a difference. The opening game is almost here.
14. That is another player introduced, and another name to follow throughout this series.
15. Preparation gives way to competition. We will find out what they bring to this matchup.

Clips 1–6 in each group are tied to their original lineup positions. Clips 7–15 work for any player.
