# Pre-match desk

Every scheduled club series has an immediate matchup briefing: team crests, selected lineups, quality comparison, both teams' recent results, tactical notes and editorial picks. Expand "Tournament journey & previous meetings" to see each completed series in this event and up to five actual head-to-head meetings, even if those meetings predate the opponent's last five results. Future results and other events are excluded from the tournament route. National matchdays use their separate event history. These use the loaded career database. There is no generated video or invented Twitch audience vote.

The existing competition introduction video plays before the matchday preview, with Skip intro and Escape available. After the video ends, is skipped, or fails to load, ordinary matches use graphics and written analysis. Majors, Worlds, EWC and FIFAe main events automatically start a short voiced discussion with visible section subtitles. Semifinals and grand finals automatically start an extended discussion. The desk mute preference persists locally and does not hide subtitles. Bracket node labels determine the stage; upper/lower finals do not count as the grand final.

The voice is **Afro American RLCS Analyst**, generated with ElevenLabs v3 during development. Playback uses the bundled MP3 library only. There are no match-time ElevenLabs requests, API keys, Python workers, GPU speech jobs, cloud credits or generation waits for the desk. The ordinary in-game commentary system is separate.

Matchday and the desk show an explicit recorded stage: Group stage with its group identifier, Round of 16, Quarterfinal, Semifinal or Final. Unknown legacy stages stay labelled as unknown or retain their recorded round; fixture numbers never manufacture a knockout stage. Upper/lower bracket finals retain those distinct names.

`data/desk-finals.json` contains 12 championship-final-only hype recordings in the same voice: four openings, four pressure sections and four closings. A final in any competition selects one of each, alongside its normal evidence-based analysis. These never appear in semifinals, groups or upper/lower bracket finals. No unrecorded crowd, venue, prize money or historical title claim is introduced by the hype scripts.

The bank includes more than 100 distinct scenario categories, with extra variations for common situations. The additional supplied 100-storyline list has three original variants per situation (300 extra recordings); see `DESK_STORYLINE_BANK.md`. The local listening catalogue is `public/audio/desk-analyst/storyline-review.html`. Numeric eligibility rules check the loaded career: lineup quality, form, streaks, recent head-to-heads, transfer activity, morale, confidence, fatigue, chemistry, attributes, series format and tournament stage. Missing evidence cannot satisfy a rule. Different fixtures select different combinations; returning to the same fixture preserves its selection. Short desks use a compact selection; extended desks add stage, form, storyline, mentality and more tactical analysis.

All database team names are baked in the same voice. The intro joins a recorded carrier phrase, the first team name, a connector and the second team name. Team-specific written graphics remain fully dynamic. Custom club names created after the bank was built use written names until an additional recording is supplied. Pronunciation aliases are retained separately from display names.

`Play now` is available after the video while the preview loads or plays, and stops playback when opening the normal player-selection screen. Navigation also stops playback. Missing files fall back to the written preview. Keys are read only by the explicit development bake scripts from `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` in their process environment. They are never saved in source, audio receipts, career saves or the release. Finished clips and digest receipts make the bake resumable without regenerating completed audio.

Bake commands (development only): `python scripts/generate-desk-bank.py --generate` and `python scripts/generate-desk-team-names.py --generate`. Without `--generate`, they report coverage without making paid requests. Name synthesis uses character timestamps to split batches into separate team recordings. Its temporary batch data lives under ignored `scratch/verification/prematch-desk/name-batches`.

## Additional clubs

The in-game six-player card showcase uses the supplied **Anticipation** music on its own Windows audio lane. It begins with the first acknowledged player card, ducks below player speech, and fades out on completion, skip, error or match transition. Its PCM recording is shipped in `public/audio/introduction-anticipation.wav`; the bridge receives the actual unpacked path in a packaged Windows release. It does not replace the analyst desk or the in-game caster audio.

`data/additional-professional-clubs.json` adds Astronic Esports (Galaxy, Mercury, insp1re), White Rabbit Gaming, T2G Esports, J.CHICKEN Esports, CHARMY and MGL eSports. Each roster has a source and tournament date. These are labelled historical tournament snapshots, not claims that their contracts are still current. Financial values, ratings for newly added identities, attributes and potential are estimates for the game.

New-career templates reuse existing player IDs, so known players are not duplicated. Existing careers receive the organizations without moving their players, resetting progression, or undoing transfers. Their roster-source note explains that the complete added lineups are available in new careers. SCR remains incomplete until a three-player lineup is confirmed.

Sources include [Astronic's April 17 lineup](https://blast.tv/rl/tournaments/rlcs-open-6-ssa-2026/series/d80bf1b0/astronic-synthesis), [SSA Open 4](https://liquipedia.net/rocketleague/Rocket_League_Championship_Series/2026/Paris_Major/Sub-Saharan_Africa/Open_4), [APAC Open 3](https://liquipedia.net/rocketleague/Rocket_League_Championship_Series/2026/Boston_Major/Asia-Pacific/Open_3), and [Telkom's September championship](https://liquipedia.net/rocketleague/VSGaming/Championships/2026).
