# Club pages and scouting

Team names in competition standings, group draws, brackets and player statistics open a club page. It shows that save's current squad, links to player profiles, club information and recent series. Player profiles link back to their club.

Manager Career has three shared scout slots for individual, regional and team assignments:

- Individual: $5,000, 28–56 days depending on observation depth.
- Region: $8,000 per month, reports every 30 days. The first month discovers up to 6 players; the second adds 10 and deepens earlier reports; the third adds 14 and revisits all earlier discoveries. In a sufficiently large unscouted region, a three-month trip covers 30 players: 6 full reports, 10 good reports and 14 basic reports. Small pools or already fully known regions yield fewer discoveries. The assignment cards show actual coverage and report depth. Full reports expose detailed skills; good reports also expose observed form.
- Team: $7,500, seven days observing the squad registered when the assignment begins. Repeat assignments deepen knowledge.

Cancel active work on the Scouting page or cancel a team assignment on its club page. Cancellation frees the slot immediately. Completed observations remain available; upfront fees are not refunded.

Every matchday automatically provides a free opponent assessment in both modes: current expected lineup, caliber and estimated rating bands, key player, strongest skill areas, relative weak areas, recorded form, recent series and preparation advice. It uses this save's roster and completed results up to the career date. These assessments do not spend money, use scout slots or unlock full recruitment knowledge. Skill summaries and advice describe roster characteristics, not promises about bot behaviour.

Manager Career additionally displays paid scouting observations, including observation dates and current roster coverage, under optional recruitment scouting. Regional scouting may discover some players without covering a whole squad. A transferred-in player does not inherit the former roster's knowledge; players who depart before a team assignment finishes are not reported. Knowledge becomes less certain after 120 days.

Paid recruitment scouting is unavailable in Path To Glory; automatic matchday assessments are available. Reports, assignments, transfers and results remain isolated per career.

## Detailed player dossiers

Click a player on the Scouting page or a recruitment team report to open their dossier. It includes observation history and days covered, estimated overall and potential, role, form, attacking skills, team play and defence, movement and decisions, mentality/personality, strengths and concerns, contract information and recorded career performance. Broad initial ranges narrow after follow-ups; unobserved details are explicitly marked as unassessed. Full reports use the saved observation, not the player's changing live hidden attributes. Overlapping observation days count once.

## AI transfers and news volume

AI clubs seek improvements to their weakest starting position, or complete an undersized squad. They submit saved offers; sellers may counter with their asking price or reject an unaffordable bid. Moves still require player interest, transfer-window eligibility, funds, salary capacity and replacement cover where necessary. Completed deals change contracts, balances and rosters in this career. AI never approves the human manager's transfers automatically and does not trade in Path To Glory.

Routine rumours, counteroffers and outcomes are collected into one transfer roundup per career day. Old same-day transfer emails are grouped when displayed, without deleting their stored messages. Offers requiring a manager decision remain separate.

Settings → Sound & gameplay → News & Chirp contains **Regional news only**. It filters transfer roundups to moves involving either club in the manager's region. Chirp's timeline, search, recommendations, trends, thread replies and notifications respect regional context. Turning it off restores worldwide coverage; world simulation and transfers continue regardless. The preference is saved in the individual career world.

SCR Esports is registered in Sub-Saharan Africa. Its 2026 2v2 participation is documented, but its current three-player roster is unconfirmed. It therefore has no fabricated starters and is not selectable for a three-player career until a roster is supplied. Financial and reputation values are game estimates.
