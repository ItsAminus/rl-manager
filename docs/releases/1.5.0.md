# RL Manager 1.5.0 — MAJOR RELEASE

A major expansion of Manager Career, national competition and match presentation. The requested version is **1.5.0**, with this release classified as **major** by its feature scope.

## Existing players

Close RL Manager and Rocket League, then run `RL.Manager.1.5.0.Setup.exe`. This is the update installer; it reuses the previously installed complete runtime. Saves and local connection settings are preserved. First-time players can use the complete 1.5.0 kit, or install the complete 1.0.8 kit and then this update. Source-code archives do not contain the bot/AI runtime or recorded voice assets.

## Career and competition changes

- National management alongside your club job: nationality-restricted three-player call-ups, separate tactics and playable FIFAe games. Group matchups play exactly two games, including 1–1 draws; knockouts are best of seven. FIFAe remains explicitly labelled as a career invitation system using the published 2025 finals format, rather than a recreation of current qualifiers.
- RLCS 2027 Club Championship with its five published stages, scoring and prize structures. Unannounced organizer inputs require an explicit career setup rather than invented official facts.
- Club debt, corporate income and agent approaches; transfers depend on sporting interest and sufficient funds. Regional scouting takes time, unknown ratings stay hidden, and contracts support additional clauses. Other clubs pursue their own moves.
- More forgiving financial dismissal: wage-limit warnings alone cannot end a career. Dismissal requires at least 180 days of unpaid obligations, six insolvent monthly settlements and a final 30-day warning. Club trophies grant 180 days of job protection. New dismissals retain a recovery archive. Club prize payments now create named, dated receipts and cannot be paid twice on retry.
- Club search by name/abbreviation and sorting by popularity, reputation, name, budget and facilities. Confirmed disbanded and incomplete squads are excluded from eligible pools. The maintainer chose to retain unverified historical entrants, clearly labelled **Last registered lineup**; the current status of every community team is not claimed to be verified.

## Match presentation

- Animated player introduction cards with portraits, career-aware honours and local car showcases before hosted matches.
- Smoother camera/showcase timing and a full-screen match-loading transition.
- Native Rocket League scoreboard integration with player identities, portraits and supported titles.
- Introduction voice-bank support, varied scripts and recorded player-name clips. Missing or unverified biographies remain clearly distinguished from verified facts.
- Trophy ceremony support and competition-specific presentation. Custom ceremony videos remain optional and may be supplied later.

## Save compatibility and limits

The update preserves existing playable career files and does not reset their contracts, balances, training, transfers or completed results. **It cannot recover career files already physically deleted by the older dismissal implementation.** Back up your existing save folder before updating. Status changes do not erase historical team records.

Private Rocket Host bot policies remain outside the game's control. Hosted availability is external. No fresh-PC live Rocket League match is claimed as release validation; automated app, bridge, native HUD and packaged-app checks are reported in the accompanying build receipt.

This remains an unsigned, unofficial Rocket League companion. The installer/update metadata and complete-kit checksums accompany the release.
