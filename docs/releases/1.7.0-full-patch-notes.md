# RLM v1.7.0 Full Patch Notes

**A major update for Rocket League Manager.** Build your own organization, discover hidden prospects, negotiate ambitious transfers and prepare for matches with a much deeper management experience.

These notes cover the changes since v1.5.2.

## Your club, your identity

- Create a custom organization when starting a **Manager Career**. Choose its region, name and abbreviation, then upload a PNG, JPG or WebP logo.
- Create three starting players and customize their names, ages and ratings without taking players from the free-agent pool.
- Your club, squad and branding belong to that save. Your uploaded logo appears throughout RLM and on the custom Rocket League match timer.
- You can continue editing your branding in **Club Studio**.

## Explore clubs and players

- Clubs now have dedicated pages with their current career squad, player links, club information and recent results.
- Click team names in competition standings, group draws, brackets and statistics to visit their club page.
- Player statistics link to player profiles, and player profiles link back to their clubs.
- Club pages reflect the transfers in your loaded career.

## Know your next opponent

- Matchday now includes a **free opponent assessment** in both Manager Career and Path To Glory.
- See the expected lineup, estimated team strength, key threat, recent form and previous meetings.
- Reports highlight strengths, potential weaknesses and practical preparation advice.
- This does not use a scouting slot or charge your club. Recruitment scouting remains separate if you want more detailed knowledge before signing someone.

## A deeper scouting network

### Choose what to scout

- Scout individual players, entire regions or a specific team.
- Regional searches support minimum/maximum ages and skill requirements, including shooting, passing, aerials, ground control, goalkeeping, positioning and boost management.
- Team scouting costs **$7,500** and takes **seven days**. Repeat assignments to build a more complete picture.
- All assignment types share three scouting slots.
- Cancel an assignment to free its slot immediately. You keep completed observations, but the upfront fee is not refunded.

### Longer trips bring more discoveries

- Regional assignments now deliver discoveries and follow-up observations as the trip progresses.
- In a large enough unscouted pool, the first month can discover six players, the second can add ten more, and the third can add fourteen more.
- Longer trips also deepen your knowledge of players already discovered. Smaller pools and previously explored regions may produce fewer new names.
- Assignment cards show your actual coverage and report depth.

### Detailed player dossiers

- Scouted players have dedicated reports covering estimated ability and potential, observed form, role, technical skills, defending, movement and decision-making.
- Learn about personality, mentality, strengths, development concerns and, with enough knowledge, contract details.
- See observation dates, report history and recorded career performances.
- Early reports give broad estimates. Follow-up work narrows them, while unassessed areas remain unknown.
- Reports can become less reliable as they age. Refresh your scouting rather than assuming an old assessment still tells the whole story.
- A team signing someone new does not automatically give you detailed knowledge of that player.

## Hidden young prospects

- Manager Careers now receive fictional grassroots prospects over time, including rare young players with exceptional potential.
- These players must be discovered through scouting. You cannot simply browse every hidden talent in the free-agent list.
- Deep reports help you judge a promising youngster, but potential remains an estimate. There is no guaranteed wonderkid in every intake.
- Recruitment quality and regional experience improve your assessments.
- High potential alone does not guarantee success: professionalism, training and development still matter.
- Some lower-rated players with previously identical attributes now have more distinct strengths and weaknesses, while retaining their overall rating.

## Chemistry and the dressing room

- **Team Play** training gradually improves player chemistry and club cohesion, including for players who have reached their development ceiling.
- Playing together helps your squad settle. A familiar starting core builds cohesion more effectively than constant changes.
- New signings need time to integrate.
- The dressing room now separates **team cohesion**, **squad atmosphere** and **support for the manager**.
- Influential squad voices and concerns about morale, trust and workload make it easier to understand the mood around your team.
- Professionalism also contributes to training development.

## Build your club behind the scenes

- Invest in three departments: **Recruitment**, **Coaching** and **Player Care**.
- Recruitment improves talent discovery and potential assessments; coaching supports training; player care improves rest-day recovery.
- Appointments take time and bring both upfront costs and monthly wages. Expansion depends on your club's stature and finances.
- Finances now includes **one-, three-, six- and twelve-month forecasts**, an operating reserve and a clearer view of available cash.
- Forecasts account for planned staff wages and scheduled transfer instalments. They are planning estimates, not guaranteed future income.

## Agree a vision with your board

- Negotiate sporting ambition, youth-development commitments and wage-budget flexibility through **Finances**.
- The board can counter your proposal. More freedom comes with expectations and trade-offs.
- Signed objectives remain in place until their annual review, alongside a longer-term reputation direction.
- Reviews consider actual results and whether young players have genuinely played. Sporting and youth assessments need a meaningful run of completed series.
- Reviews affect board confidence and can adjust your wage budget. You receive the outcome in your inbox.
- Agreed wage limits also matter when seeking transfer approval.

## Transfers that take negotiation

### Deal with the selling club

- Negotiate the transfer package with the club before discussing personal terms.
- Sellers weigh the player's importance, availability, contract situation, morale and their own finances.
- Clubs can counter, reject or end unsuccessful talks. You can also withdraw.
- Accepted club agreements expire after seven days and do not guarantee the player will join.

### Build relationships with agents

- Players are represented by fictional agencies with different negotiating attitudes.
- Relationships carry across clients represented by the same agency within your career.
- Successful deals help your relationship; collapsed negotiations hurt it.
- Negotiation patience persists when you leave and reopen the page. Repeated poor offers can end talks and trigger a cooldown.
- Players still consider sporting interest, their role, contract length and the overall offer. Money alone does not guarantee a signature.

### Spread the fee

- Pay in full or negotiate **three-, six- or twelve-month instalment deals** with an upfront payment.
- Sellers may demand a larger upfront share or a higher total fee for deferred payment.
- The deal screen separates the full transfer fee, immediate payment, deferred balance, signing bonus and wages.
- Your board considers future commitments when assessing affordability.
- Finances shows outgoing and incoming instalments. Scheduled obligations continue even if a player later leaves the club.

### A busier football-style transfer market

- AI clubs pursue improvements to weak starting positions and fill incomplete squads.
- Offers can become negotiations, counters, completed transfers or collapsed talks, rather than just rumours.
- Clubs still need an open transfer window, sufficient funds and player interest.
- Completed deals update squads and contracts throughout your career.
- Path To Glory retains its fixed-squad structure and remains outside this transfer market.

## More news, fewer emails

- Routine transfer stories are collected into a **daily roundup**, including rumours, counters, completed moves and collapsed negotiations.
- Offers and other decisions needing your attention remain separate.
- Transfer-news links open the relevant Chirp transfer search.
- Enable **Regional news only** in Settings to focus transfer coverage and Chirp on your career's region.
- The filter also covers Chirp searches, recommendations, trends, replies and notifications. Other regions continue playing and trading in the background.
- Turn it off whenever you want worldwide coverage again.

## A better matchday broadcast

- Instant matchup graphics and written analysis use the selected lineups, recent results, key players, previous meetings and tournament journey.
- Ordinary fixtures receive a quick written briefing. Major events can include a short voiced analyst desk, with longer coverage for semifinals and finals.
- Competition introduction videos play before the desk again.
- Desk audio starts automatically when the video finishes or is skipped, with subtitles and a persistent mute preference.
- **Play now / Skip preview** remains available so you can get straight to the match.
- Missing recordings fall back to written coverage. Starting a match or leaving the page stops the desk audio.
- National-team match previews use their own international results.
- Group matches identify the group, and knockout matches clearly show stages such as Round of 16, quarterfinal, semifinal and final.
- Championship finals receive exclusive hype recordings. Upper/lower bracket finals keep their own labels.

### Expanded recorded audio

- Added hundreds of analyst recordings covering underdogs, rematches, form, pressure and other match situations, plus final-exclusive lines.
- Added an extensive recorded club and national-team name library.
- The desk plays prepared recordings offline, without generating speech or spending voice-service credits at matchday.
- Storylines depend on the information available in your career; a recording existing does not mean its story applies to every matchup.
- Custom club names appear on screen, but arbitrary newly entered names are not automatically added to the recorded voice library.
- Added background music for the in-game player-card introductions, with speech ducking and clean stopping when the sequence ends or is skipped.

## More local tactical choices

For supported **Easy/Medium local bots**, additional tactical controls include:

- Close, balanced or deep support positioning.
- Narrow, balanced or wide passing outlets.
- Demo targeting focused on the star player, nearest opponent or last defender.
- Boost-reserve choices of 0, 15 or 30.

These settings affect the local tactical controls around the bots, with safeguards for kickoffs, aerial play and emergencies. They do not rewrite Rocket Host bots' private policies in Hard mode.

The Hard-mode ladder now spreads stronger policies across the upper ratings more gradually:

| 99 | Kiyo3 |
| 98 | Chronos |
| 97 | BonkDaddy |
| 96 | Byte |
| 93–95 | OptiGP, Opti, RippleV1, London |
| 90–92 | NextoPlus, Ducky |
| 89 | AstraV5, Daizen, Zeus, OptiFR |
| 86–88 | AstraV5, Daizen, Zeus |
| 79–85 | Necto |

- Ratings below 79 and the Easy/Medium and Path To Glory tier definitions are unchanged by this rebalance.
- Available healthy bots are used; known failing policies can be excluded. London remains subject to its existing reliability safeguard.
- If a preferred bot is unavailable, RLM tries suitable healthy alternatives and explains substitutions beside the affected player.
- Healthy assignments remain fixed for the series.
- One unavailable bot no longer makes every player appear unassigned. If an essential slot still cannot be filled, RLM blocks the launch with a specific explanation.
- Rocket Host's Necto is advertised as Champ; it is not the local Diamond policy. The assignment preview makes that distinction clear.

## Steam, BakkesMod and match reliability

- Repaired RLM's Steam launch route while retaining the required offline-match launch options.
- RLM automatically adds the required BakkesMod command permissions while preserving unrelated settings.
- Updated bridge files are checked and refreshed more reliably, including missing or stale files.
- Existing saves, preferences and runtime state are preserved during those repairs.
- Updated users do not need the separate Steam support ZIP or manual command-file editing for these changes.

**Hard mode remains experimental.** Rocket Host availability, injection and performance can vary by PC. The Steam repair addresses the diagnosed launch issue, but cannot guarantee every machine-specific problem is resolved.

## Audio controls and readability

- Added a dedicated **0–100% commentator volume slider**, alongside mute.
- It controls recorded and generated commentators, player introductions, post-game discussion and the pre-match analyst desk.
- Crowd reactions, countdown audio and interface effects remain separate.
- In-match volume changes apply to the next played game.
- Fixed attribute values blending into their coloured backgrounds on player profiles, making ratings easier to read across rating tiers.

## More teams and SSA coverage

- Expanded professional organization coverage with Astronic Esports, White Rabbit Gaming, T2G Esports, J.CHICKEN Esports, CHARMY and MGL eSports, plus an SCR Esports registration.
- Added missing SSA clubs from the community roster list: The Punishers, Roumds, Marine Region, 7x7, LFO, The Wave, Stanislas, Team Espionage and Fusion.
- Added supplied free-agent entries where appropriate, avoiding duplicate known identities.
- Community and dated rosters are labelled accordingly. Existing career transfers are respected, and substitutes remain separate from starting lineups.
- SCR's roster remains incomplete pending a confirmed lineup. Clubs without a confirmed logo use a fallback crest.

## Installing v1.7.0

**Already playing RLM?** Close RLM and Rocket League, then install **RL.Manager.1.7.0.Setup.exe**. Keep your existing runtime components; your saves and settings remain in place.

**First installation?** Follow the [README installation guide](../../README.md), install the complete setup components, then install the v1.7.0 application update. The existing complete runtime kit remains the base download for this release.

RLM is a Windows application. Follow the prerequisite and Rocket League launch-option instructions in the installation guide before starting a match.
