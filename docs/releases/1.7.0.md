## RLM v1.7.0 Update — MAJOR UPDATE RELEASE!

RLM v1.7.0 brings deeper management systems, improved match presentation and important reliability fixes.

[Read the RLM v1.7.0 Full Patch Notes](https://github.com/ItsAminus/rl-manager/blob/codex/public-release/docs/releases/1.7.0-full-patch-notes.md)

- **Expanded scouting:** detailed player dossiers, age/attribute filters, team scouting, cancellable assignments and better results from longer scouting trips.
- **Hidden young prospects ("Wonderkids"):** discover rare high-potential players through scouting, with uncertain potential estimates and no guaranteed wonderkids.
- **Deeper club management:** recruitment, coaching and player-care departments, improved chemistry development, dressing-room dynamics and financial forecasts.
- **Negotiated board visions:** agree sporting, youth-development and spending objectives, with annual reviews.
- **More realistic transfers:** selling-club bargaining, counteroffers, persistent agent relationships and 3/6/12-month instalment deals.
- **A more active transfer market:** AI clubs pursue squad improvements, negotiate offers and complete real transfers.
- **Less inbox clutter:** daily transfer roundups and an optional regional filter covering transfer news and Chirp.
- **Custom organizations:** create a Manager Career club in your region, customize three starting players and their ratings, and upload a logo used in RLM and the match timer.
- **Improved matchday presentation:** automatic opponent reports, clearer tournament-stage labels, skippable analyst discussions with subtitles, finals-exclusive commentary and expanded recorded voice coverage.
- **Hard-mode rebalance:** a more gradual bot-strength ladder, clearer fallback explanations and a fix for one unavailable bot making everyone appear unassigned.
- **Steam/BakkesMod repairs:** corrected Steam launching, automatic RCON command setup and more reliable updates to installed match-bridge files.
- **Audio and readability:** a dedicated commentator volume slider and fixes for unreadable player attribute numbers.
- **More teams and navigation:** additional SSA/APAC organizations, new SSA roster entries, and clickable club/player pages across competitions and statistics.

### Download and update

**Existing players:** close RL Manager and Rocket League, then install `RL.Manager.1.7.0.Setup.exe`. Keep your existing runtime, saves and settings. No separate Steam patch ZIP, PowerShell command or manual RCON config edit is required.

**New players:** install all four parts of the [complete 1.5.0 kit](https://github.com/ItsAminus/rl-manager/releases/tag/v1.5.0) first, then run the **1.7.0 app installer**. The large bot/model runtime is reused; the 1.7.0 installer contains the new application, bridge, data, HUD assets and recorded desk audio. [Installation instructions](https://github.com/ItsAminus/rl-manager#start-here-first-time-installation).

### Current limits

- Hard/Rocket Host remains experimental. The corrected Steam launch route is regression-tested, but successful injection still depends on the player's Steam/BakkesMod environment. Keep Steam running and signed in.
- Rocket Host advertises its Necto as Champ; the local Diamond policy cannot be mixed into a hosted match. Bot-health exclusions still apply, including London's existing failure safeguard.
- Custom club names are displayed correctly; arbitrary new names do not receive an automatic recording from the pre-generated name bank.
- Scouting, negotiation, staffing and board rules are original RLM adaptations, not an exact copy of Football Manager's private formulas.
